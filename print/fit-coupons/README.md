# Tilt-interface fit coupons

Two 44 mm discs with the same revision 3, 72-tooth profiles and 5.3 mm bores.
Import in millimetres. Both files are already oriented with teeth facing up.
The female disc is 4 mm thick; the male is 4.45 mm including its tooth relief.

Use the intended filament and 0.10–0.12 mm layers through the teeth. Verify the
slicer preserves each ridge. Bring the faces together by hand: they should seat
at 5° increments and disengage when separated axially. Check for rocking,
incomplete seating and damaged crests. Do not force or sand away the tooth form.

Passing this fit check does not establish the complete mount's load capacity.
